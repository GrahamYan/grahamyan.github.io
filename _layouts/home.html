About Me

I am Ge Yan (严格), currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. My research interests lie at the fascinating intersection of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Quantum Computing. In particular, my work primarily focuses on the synergy of machine learning algorithms and Quantum Error Correction (QEC) to build fault-tolerant quantum systems.

Before joining NTU, I obtained my Ph.D. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in 2025, where I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Junchi Yan. Prior to that, I received my Bachelor's degree from the prestigious ACM Honors Class at Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2020.
